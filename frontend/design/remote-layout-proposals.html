Remote Operation — layout proposals

Interactive mockups. Everything with a cursor does something: mode pills swap the control card, drawers open, the stage/PIP swaps on click (A & D), Connect toggles the fake link, section tabs switch (C), and E-STOP latches — press P (or click it again) to reset, just like the real keybinding.

A — Cockpit: video-first with an overlay HUD

Full-bleed width. Video stays the hero: status, vitals and E-STOP float over the feed as a HUD, one right rail holds controls, and dataset/policy/telemetry/logs are a bottom drawer bar. Click the PIP to swap video ⇄ 3D.
